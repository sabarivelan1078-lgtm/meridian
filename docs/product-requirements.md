# Personal IELTS General Speaking Coach - Product Requirements

## Product vision
Build a personal Android tablet app that helps a Tamil-speaking IELTS General Training learner improve IELTS Speaking performance toward Band 8 through daily 15-minute adaptive practice, accurate rubric-based feedback, and premium tablet-first UX.

The app is a personal product and is not intended for Play Store release. It should prioritize usefulness, privacy, smoothness, and learning quality over public marketplace features.

## Target user
- IELTS General Training candidate.
- Speaking is the first priority; Listening, Reading, and Writing can be added later.
- Mother tongue: Tamil.
- Device: Redmi Pad 2.
- Development constraint: the learner does not know coding and does not have a PC.
- Available study time: 15 minutes per day.
- Preferred style: English explanations with Tamil support.
- Coaching tone: balanced IELTS examiner plus friendly personal coach.
- Visual theme: premium dark mode first.

## Core outcomes
The app should help the learner:
1. Understand their current IELTS Speaking level.
2. Receive a realistic estimated band score using IELTS-style criteria.
3. Follow a customized 100-day plan aimed at Band 8 speaking habits.
4. Practice every day in short, focused 15-minute sessions.
5. Improve fluency, pronunciation, vocabulary, grammar, coherence, and confidence.
6. Receive active feedback after speaking tasks.
7. Store recordings, transcripts, feedback, and progress when useful for training.
8. Recover gracefully after missed days.
9. Use Google AI Pro ecosystem features where practical.

## Product principles
- Speaking-first: every major feature must support spoken performance.
- IELTS-rubric-aligned: scoring and feedback must map to IELTS Speaking criteria.
- Tamil-supported, English-led: Tamil clarifies hard concepts, but practice stays English-first.
- Adaptive: the plan should change based on performance, missed days, and recurring weaknesses.
- Personal and private: no social features, no public publishing, and no Play Store workflow required.
- Premium and smooth: the app should feel polished, fast, gesture-friendly, and tablet-native.
- No-PC delivery: development, testing instructions, and APK production must assume the learner only has a Redmi Pad 2.

## In scope for MVP
- Onboarding and learner profile.
- IELTS General Speaking diagnostic test.
- Estimated band scoring by criteria.
- 100-day adaptive plan.
- Daily 15-minute lesson flow.
- Voice recording and local storage.
- Transcription pipeline.
- AI feedback in English with Tamil support.
- Band 8 answer improvement suggestions.
- Progress dashboard.
- Missed-day recovery logic.
- Premium dark mode UI direction.
- Redmi Pad 2 tablet optimization.
- APK build pipeline for personal installation.
- Plain-English APK install and testing guide for a non-coder.

## Out of scope for MVP
- Play Store release.
- Public user accounts or social sharing.
- Tutor marketplace.
- Full Reading, Writing, and Listening modules.
- Payment systems.
- Multiplayer challenges.
- Heavy gamification that distracts from IELTS progress.

## Success metrics
- The learner completes at least 5 practice sessions per week.
- The app provides clear feedback within one session flow.
- The learner can see progress across all four IELTS Speaking criteria.
- Missed days do not break the plan; they trigger rebalancing.
- The app can produce an installable APK without requiring the learner to own a PC.
- The learner can follow release notes and test the APK without using terminal commands.
- The UI remains smooth and readable on Redmi Pad 2 in portrait and landscape.
